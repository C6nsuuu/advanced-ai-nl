# Rolex condition grading

**Bron:** Bob's Watches, "Rolex Condition Grading", https://www.bobswatches.com/help/rolex-condition-grading
**Opgehaald:** 2026-11-24
**Wat:** alleen de uitleg over de staat (condition grades), letterlijk overgenomen.

---

Bob's Watches grades the condition of our watches based on the following list of terms.

Unworn - Mint condition with no signs of wear.
Excellent - A watch in this condition will show very slight signs of wear.
Very Good - Light to normal wear by a careful owner.
Good - This watch shows signs of wear and may have some minor scratches or marks on the case, bezel, or bracelet. However, it remains in proper working order and retains its original integrity. A great choice for someone who values function and history over purely aesthetic appeal.
Vintage - These watches are generally 30 years or older and condition can vary.
